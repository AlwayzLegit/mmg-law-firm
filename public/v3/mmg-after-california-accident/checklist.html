After a California accident

Safety and medical needs come first. General checklist for car, truck, and motorcycle collisions.

  • Stop and address immediate danger. Call 911 for injuries or an emergency.
  • Exchange driver, vehicle, insurance, and witness details when safe.
  • Save scene photographs, original videos, report number, and tow details.
  • Obtain appropriate medical care; keep instructions and bills.
  • Check the 24-hour law-enforcement reporting requirement for injuries or death.
  • File a DMV SR-1 within 10 days when injury, death, or over $1,000 damage to any one person’s property triggers it.
  • Notify your insurer promptly and check policy requirements.
  • Back up original files; save letters, receipts, damaged gear, and camera locations.
  • Ask about preservation before repair, disposal, or transfer; address storage fees.
  • Check your specific legal deadlines promptly. Contacting a lawyer does not extend them.
